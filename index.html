Who We Are

At Valleytown Tutoring, we provide a nurturing and supportive environment where students are engaged one-on-one with a private tutor. Our tutors are experts in their fields and mentor students of all ages. We offer tutoring across a wide variety of subjects, including college prep and GED preparation. Our facility provides both Mac and Windows computer labs loaded with the current operating systems, and we are stocked with over 100 textbooks commonly used in public and private schools. Check with your tutor before lugging your books as we may already have a copy of what you use.

We have been offering tutoring services to Valleytown and surrounding areas since 1998. While our tutors have changed over the years, we strive to employ not only subject matter experts, but experts who understand the learning process.

Who We Teach

We teach students of all ages from public and private school k-12, home schoolers, college students, and even adult students. We cover everything from learning to count through AP coursework and SAT/ACT preparation.

What We Teach

Other tutors may say they teach math, science, and other subjects. But at Valleytown Tutoring, we teach students. We employ the Socratic method to stimulate our students and lead them towards figuring out answers for themselves rather than simply spoon feeding answers. Don't worry, this doesn't mean our students are teaching themselves! By discovering solutions and methods on their own (with guidance), students gain a richer understanding of the material. Students are never left floundering with problems or questions that are beyond them.
