Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I come?

We recommend twice a week for one to two hours per visit, depending on your subject area need. The key to mastering any skill is repetition and practice.

Do I have to come twice a week?

No.

While we recommend twice a week for one to two hours per session, you can come as little or as often as you like. Once you purchase a tutoring package, you are free to spread our your purchased hours any way you like.

Do you make house calls?

No.

We believe students do better when they make an effort to travel to a dedicated learning environment free from distractions.

How much does it cost?

Sessions are $40/hour booked in four hour blocks.

How do I get started?

  1. Call us to book your free assessment session.
  2. Schedule a free follow-up session to review your assessment results.
  3. Choose one of the recommended tutors and plan your schedule.
  4. Pay by check or credit card the day of your first session.

Where can I get free tutoring?

There are several reputable free learning sites. While they offer lessons or courses that can help guide you through a difficult topic, they lack the immediacy of mentor feedback. Still, they are valuable resources and can help to fill in some learning gaps.

  • Khan Academy
  • Coursera
  • Harvard, MIT, Berkeley, and more
